About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Textile Accessories

Welcome to SABR Accessories, a premier supplier of garment and textile accessories based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. With deep industry expertise and a commitment to quality, we serve garment factories, buying houses, and exporters worldwide.

From sewing threads to packaging solutions, we provide everything you need to keep your production running smoothly — at competitive prices with reliable delivery.
